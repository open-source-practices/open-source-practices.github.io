## Who am I?
I'm Arielle Bennett, currently Senior Researcher in Open Source Practices at The Alan Turing Institute. 
I'm a contributor to open source projects such as The Turing Way, JupyterHub, and JupyterBook. 
My career to date spans over a decade in research, science & innovation spaces, from open access journals, to neuroscience community management. 
I love digging into the underlying systems, motivations, and barriers to participation in different spaces, whether that's contributing to open source or responsible AI adoption.
